Privacy Policy — Open Wheel Pit Wall

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Open Wheel Pit Wall ("the Application") is an independent motorsport telemetry, live timing, and spatial pit wall visualization application for Android XR, tablets, foldables, phones, and Chromebooks. We are committed to protecting your privacy: the Application operates with zero developer backend servers and does not collect, store, track, or sell any personal data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your name, email address, phone number, physical location, device identifiers, or usage analytics on any developer-owned server.
  • The Application does not include any third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or telemetry/analytics trackers.

2. Optional Third-Party Account Linking (F1 TV® SSO)

  • Core features of the Application—including historical Grand Prix telemetry replays, 2D/3D circuit maps, and live timing towers—work without signing into any account.
  • If you optionally choose to link your personal F1 TV® Pro subscription to play synchronized commentary audio or onboard video feeds, authentication occurs directly between your device's Android System WebView and Formula 1's official Single Sign-On portal (https://account.formula1.com).
  • Your password is never read, intercepted, or stored by the Application. The resulting session entitlement token is encrypted strictly on your local device using the Android Hardware Keystore (AES-256-GCM) and is excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.
  • You can unlink your session and purge all local WebView cookies and stored tokens at any time using the "Unlink Account" button inside the Application or by clearing the Application's storage in Android Settings.

3. External Telemetry & Media Endpoints

To render live and historical motorsport timing data, your device makes direct HTTPS/WSS requests to:

  • OpenF1 API (https://api.openf1.org): Open-source historical telemetry, lap timings, pit stops, and car position data licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
  • Formula 1 Live Timing & Media Streams (*.formula1.com): Direct live timing feeds and (when authenticated with an active subscriber account) Widevine DRM media streams.

4. Android XR & Device Permissions

  • android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to stream motorsport timing data and authorized media feeds.
  • android.permission.HEAD_TRACKING (Android XR devices only): Used locally by the Android XR Jetpack SDK (FollowingSubspace) to keep the 3D spatial HUD comfortably aligned with your field of view in Full Space mode. No head-tracking or spatial sensor data ever leaves your device.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
